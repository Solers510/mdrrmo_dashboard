# OMDRRMO Naic Visual Identity

## Official branding files

The source-of-truth application assets are:

- `assets/branding/OMDRRMO.png`
- `assets/branding/bayannaic.png`

These filenames are preserved exactly. Phase 11A does not rename, resize,
recompress, recolor, or overwrite the supplied files.

## Alert-level rule

Application semantic colors and official disaster alert levels are not
interchangeable. An official alert must always display its textual name in
addition to color. Final operational alert definitions remain subject to
MDRRMO verification.

## Layout direction

Dark institutional shell + light operational workspace.
